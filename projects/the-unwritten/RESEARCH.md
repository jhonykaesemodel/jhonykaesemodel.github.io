# The Unwritten — research and media ledger

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

This exhibit is an evidence-led introduction, not a claim to final historical truth. Dates are rounded for readability in the interface; uncertainty and model limits are disclosed in the app. “Oldest known” means the oldest surviving evidence currently described by the cited source, not the date an ability was invented.

## Claim ledger

| Exhibit claim | Evidence source | Strength and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cuneiform originated in Mesopotamia before 3200 BCE and early tablets often recorded accounting | [British Museum — How to write cuneiform](https://www.britishmuseum.org/blog/how-write-cuneiform) | Strong for surviving cuneiform. The exhibit calls it one of the earliest writing traditions; it does not claim one uncontested global “first writing.” |
| Jebel Irhoud materials date to 315 ± 34 thousand years ago | [Nature — New fossils from Jebel Irhoud](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature22335) | Directly cited dating range. 315 ka is a reference point, not a birthday for the species. |
| Jebel Irhoud preserves a mosaic of modern facial/dental traits and more primitive cranial form | [Nature — The age of the hominin fossils from Jebel Irhoud](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature22336) | Supports a population-level, mosaic emergence of Homo sapiens rather than a single “first human.” |
| Deliberate abstract drawing is preserved in 73 ka levels at Blombos Cave | [Nature — An abstract drawing from the 73,000-year-old levels at Blombos Cave](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0514-3) | Strong for deliberate marking. Meaning or symbolic interpretation is not recoverable from the marks alone. The photograph used in the exhibit is a related engraved ochre piece, explicitly not the exact drawn fragment in this paper. |
| Musical instruments were established in Ice Age Europe by roughly 40 ka | [University of Tübingen — Hohle Fels excavations](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/faculties/faculty-of-science/departments/geosciences/work-groups/prehistory-and-archaeological-sciences/ina/early-prehistory-quaternary-ecology/research/excavations/germany/hohle-fels/) and [Nature, doi:10.1038/nature08169](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08169) | The instrument survives; its repertoire, performance practice, and emotional meaning do not. The exhibit image shows a museum reconstruction. |
| In-cave burning occurred at Wonderwerk Cave around 1 Ma | [PNAS — Microstratigraphic evidence of in situ fire](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1117620109) | Microscopic ash and burned bone support in-situ burning. This is not presented as the invention of fire or proof of fire-making. |
| Lucy (AL 288-1) dates to about 3.18 Ma and combines bipedal and climbing adaptations | [Smithsonian Human Origins — AL 288-1](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/human-fossils/fossils/al-288-1) and [Smithsonian — Dating](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/dating) | Strong for age and anatomy. The exhibit does not call Lucy a direct ancestor or Homo sapiens. The image is a museum skeletal reconstruction. |
| The origin and early chronology of spoken language remain unresolved | [Antiquity — The archaeology of language origins: a review](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/antiquity/article/abs/archaeology-of-language-originsa-review/4B053CBE2C6D633D3345CE8EE157BB78) | Language itself does not fossilize. Anatomy, genetics, tools, and social evidence provide indirect constraints, not a recoverable first sentence. The interface’s 150 ka position is deliberately labeled “date unknown” and serves as a visual prompt, not a dated claim. |

## Scale calculations

- Homo sapiens reference span: 315,000 years, matching the central Jebel Irhoud estimate while retaining the ±34,000-year range in the app.
- Surviving cuneiform writing reference: about 5,200 years.
- Writing span as a fraction of the species reference: `5,200 / 315,000 = 1.6508%`.
- If 315,000 years are compressed to 24 hours, 5,200 years occupy 23.77 minutes and an 80-year life occupies 21.94 seconds.
- “Generation” counts use 25 years only as a scale analogy, not a claim that human generation length was constant.

## Deliberate omissions and boundaries

- The exhibit does not date the invention of fire, language, music, symbolic thought, or writing from a single artifact.
- It does not imply that cultural or cognitive change was linear.
- It does not state that a baby 300,000 years ago had every capability of a child today. Both belong to populations recognized as Homo sapiens, but individual cognition, language, development, culture, nutrition, and childhood experience are not recoverable at that precision.
- The human-family scale ends at 3.3 Ma to include Lucy and the earliest presently known stone-tool evidence as a visual boundary. The tool evidence itself is not a featured evidence card in v1.
